# Reusable captions

## Short

Competing requests. One explainable plan.

Meet RailPlan: a rail-maintenance planning prototype that helps schedulers inspect
conflicts, explore trade-offs and share a traceable plan. Built for Nebula X PS1.

## Longer

Maintenance, upgrades and renewals compete for the same short engineering night.
Moving one request can create another clash across track access, equipment or
workforce capacity.

RailPlan brings those trade-offs into view: reviewed requests, deterministic
constraint checks, inspectable schedules and versioned handover to contractors.
AI can assist with intake and explanations; planners own the decisions.

We're building this as a non-operational prototype using fabricated planning
inputs, and our next step is validating the assumptions with real planning needs.
